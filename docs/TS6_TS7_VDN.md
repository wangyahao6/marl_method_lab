# TS6 and TS7 VDN training, 2026-11-07

The two suites use the exact Protoss rosters in `configs/continual/ts6.json` and `configs/continual/ts7.json`. Both use ten 2M thresholds and switch after a whole episode. The 10gen Protoss encoding remains Stalker=0, Zealot=1, Colossus=2, with observation/state/action dimensions 92/130/11. The denominator for reward scaling follows the enemy health and shields each reset.

The batch compares baseline and A2 overall top-3 historical union at seeds 1234, 4567 and 3535. Protected updates use scale 0.1. At each trained boundary the latest 32 training episodes supply the next selection. TS6 restores its method-matched TS3 E checkpoint and the protected A2 set, then samples 32 episodes on E with the E policy and its cumulative epsilon to extend the union before F. This sample uses its own seed and environment and does not enter training replay or advance the training budget. TS6 restores online/target parameters, optimizer and counters. Replay starts empty, RNG is reseeded, and the last target refresh is inferred from the original episode schedule. TS7 shares one A prefix per seed and restores replay, optimizer, counters, recoverable RNG and the first boundary selection for the paired branches.

Evaluation uses the TS3 task-index seed blocks. The 128-episode stage evaluations cover seen tasks and the next unseen task. TS6 reuses A–E stage-end evaluations and obtains protected-branch pre-training points for C–E from the corresponding historical checkpoints. The initial model is reconstructed with the historical seed and initializer. TS6 reuses historical initial evaluations when the reconstructed hashes agree and collects a common set of initial evaluations otherwise. TS7 evaluates its original untrained agent. Each completed path writes `retention.json` with the matrix and ACC/BWT/FWT by stage.

The submitted manifest is `results/ts6_ts7_vdn_20261107/plan.json`. It lists the six TS6 continuations, three TS7 shared prefixes and six dependent TS7 B–J branches. Training and evaluation artifacts live under `runs/<run-id>/`.
