# Authorized matched-count type-conditioned ablation, 2026-11-01

Entry scripts/type_matched_ablation.py; summary scripts/summarize_type_ablation.py. Three existing final pure-QMIX 2s3z models (1111,2222,3535), layers A1/A2 separately. Exact inputs and SHA-256 in results/2s3z_type_matched_ablation_20261101/provenance.json. No new training/environment sampling, no saved-model modification.

Each final 32-training-episode window is split by episode index: even indices 0..30 for screening (16), odd indices 1..31 for evaluation (16). This is held-out evaluation within the recent behavior-policy training window, not independent deployment rollouts. All selection scores are recomputed exclusively on screening episodes, using fixed original-model c/mean(c), absolute activations and group-local layer mean normalization, including original no-op observations. k is source-type strict score>3 count. k=0 skips comparison. High/low overall rank controls use k even when not >3. Ascending neuron index breaks score ties. No-mask baseline is exempt from equal mask counts. Random uses 20 sets sampled without replacement, reused identically across the two target species; draws are not training seeds.

Each set acts only on target-type activation rows at the chosen layer, throughout complete episode replay; GRU memory evolves under the intervention. Observations and previous actions remain from recorded trajectories. Thus results measure open-loop history-conditioned decision sensitivity, not win-rate change, counterfactual environment trajectories or continual-learning efficacy. Both species receive the same source sets separately. Two source-species comparisons can have different k, so do not treat raw effects across different k as a fair type-specificity contrast.

Evaluation uses valid non-bootstrap decision moments with at least two available actions, separately for the same target-type rows. Metrics: legal greedy-action flip (argmax first-index tie rule); absolute and signed change in original-greedy-action Q minus strongest other legal Q; legal-coordinate Q MAE. This original-action margin can turn negative after intervention. Baseline near-tie fraction also saved. Record episode-level and pooled metrics, not independent timestep inference. Screening/evaluation all-zero response fractions are separately recorded for each target type. Overlap of type high and equal-k overall high, plus separately matched exclusive-set interventions, are included; exclusive sets have their own equal size, not k.

Validation: all primary matched mask sizes checked; original no-mask replay has zero effects; only target species changes, other species outputs exactly match baseline; fresh no-mask replay exactly recovers baseline; model parameter hashes unchanged. Result: 12 candidate screening conditions, 1 k=0 skip; 11 comparable conditions, 2 target types each; 586 case/repeat rows including controls and exclusive sets. No claims from random draws as extra experimental seeds. Report/plots under results/2s3z_type_matched_ablation_20261101.
